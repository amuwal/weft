Common questions

How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all subscription and in-app purchase refunds. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Weft → Cancel, then request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple denies it and the reason feels unfair, email hello@getweft.xyz and we'll back you up.
I lost my notes. Can you recover them?
If iCloud sync was enabled, reinstall Weft on any device signed into the same Apple ID — your data syncs back automatically. If sync was off and you deleted the app, the local data is gone. Weft is on-device by design; nothing ever leaves your iCloud, so we never had a copy to recover.
How do I restore a purchase after reinstalling?
Inside Weft, open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy. Premium activates within a few seconds.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Weft → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase — there's nothing to cancel.
iCloud sync isn't working. What can I check?
Three things to check, in order: (1) the same Apple ID is signed in on every device; (2) iCloud Drive is on in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud; (3) inside Weft, Settings → iCloud sync is enabled. Sync requires Premium. After flipping any of these, relaunch Weft once.
Does Weft work on Android or the web?
No. Weft is iOS-only and uses native Apple frameworks (SwiftData, CloudKit, WidgetKit) end to end. There are no plans for Android or web. If you switch platforms, you can export your data from Settings → Export PDF or Markdown.
What data does Weft collect?
None on our servers. Your notes live on your device and in your iCloud, never in our infrastructure. We have no analytics, no telemetry, no third-party SDKs. The full breakdown is in our privacy policy.
How long until I hear back?
Usually within a day or two — sometimes faster. Weft is built by one person in Tokyo; the same person answers email. If you don't hear back within a week, your message was eaten by a spam filter — please resend to hello@getweft.xyz.
